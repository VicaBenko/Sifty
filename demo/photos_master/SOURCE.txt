Demo photo set - 128 images from the COCO 2017 dataset (coco128 sample,
distributed by Ultralytics: https://github.com/ultralytics/yolov5).

COCO images are sourced from Flickr under Creative Commons licences and are
widely used as a public research benchmark. They are used here as a neutral,
public stand-in for a personal photo gallery, so that no private photos are
shown during the demo.

Everyday multi-object scenes - people, animals, food, furniture, tableware,
vehicles - which is exactly the material the Sift architecture is built to
query.
